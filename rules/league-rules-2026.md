# Plunk Cup — League House Rules (2026)

Proposed by Paul Spetrini for the 2026 season.

## 1. Keepers
- 2 max keepers.
- A player can be kept twice (3-year total lifespan).
- Cost rises 1 round from the original draft pick each year.
- Undrafted player: costs a 7th, then a 6th.
- **Dropping and re-adding a drafted player does not make him "undrafted"** — his drafted-round cost follows him all season. The 7th-round cost is only for players never drafted this year.
- Trading a keeper does not reset his keeper clock.
- Eligibility: drafted round 5 or later (or undrafted).
- **If you don't hold the pick a keeper costs**, either acquire that round's pick in a pre-draft trade (pick trades are uncapped) or pay with the nearest higher pick you own — your 3rd for a 4th-round cost. Never a later/cheaper pick.
- **Each keeper burns its own pick.** Two keepers costing the same round require two picks in that round (two 7ths = two undrafted keeps). With only one, the second keeper escalates to your nearest higher pick — a 7th **and** a 6th.

## 2. Trade Lockups
- Players may be traded through the week before playoffs.
- Round 1–2 future picks cannot be traded or acquired.
- **Weekly deadline:** player trades are due by midnight the night before the earliest game involving any traded player. Thursday game = submitted by Wednesday midnight.
- **Pick-only trades have no deadline.**

### 2A. Veto Process
- **4 veto votes** from managers not in the trade sends it to review — same threshold for any trade size.
- On review, the non-vetoing managers confer by phone, hear the perspectives, and make the final call.
- Please do not abuse the veto.

### 2B. The Right to Tank
- Keeper league, big buy-in — trading away players within the rules is your right.
- A clearly lopsided or collusive trade can still be rejected via the veto.
- Teams must field active players (**regular season only**), barring bye-week shortages.
- Violations: the commissioner picks two managers not in the matchup to form a 3-person committee that resolves the issue.

## 3. Waiver
- FAB budget system: $100 per manager per season, $1 minimum bid.
- Claim ties are broken by the league's rolling waiver priority list.

## 4. Reset (2025 only)
- **One-time format for the 2025 soft reset — does not apply going forward.**
- Fixed-order draft rounds 1–4 by last season's ranking, snake from round 5 — a compromise between a full reset and none.
- **2026 onward uses #5 below.**

## 5. Draft Order (2026 onward)
- **Rounds 1–2:** fixed **reverse-standings** order — worst team picks first, champion last, in both rounds (no snake-back).
- **Round 3 onward:** normal snake.
- **Example (8 teams):** champion picks 1.08, 2.08, 3.01; last place picks 1.01, 2.01, 3.08.
- **Why:** two straight reverse rounds give the worst team a real leg-up (1.01 + 2.01); the snake keeps the rest balanced; contenders get the 2.08 → 3.01 turn.
- A keeper forfeits that team's pick in its cost round; teams skip rounds their keepers occupy.
- Optional anti-tank guardrail (TBD by vote): weighted lottery among non-playoff teams for the top of the order.

## 6. Player Auto-Subs
We're all busy people — the league uses Sleeper's built-in **Player Auto-Subs** ([how it works](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/9731991-how-does-player-autosubs-work)) instead of manual swaps.

- An **inactive** starter is auto-replaced by his designated bench sub.
- Assign subs in the mobile app; no injury tag required.
- The sub must fit the starter's slot; each sub covers one starter.
- Locks once either player's game starts; change subs any time before.
- **League setting: 2 auto-subs per week.**

## 7. Payouts
$300 buy-in per manager, **due before the draft** — $2,400 pot.

- **$20 per win** × 14 regular-season weeks ($1,120)
- **$380** for most points scored
- **$900** for the league champion
- **Ties** in any payout stat split evenly among tied managers.

Weekly wins spread the wealth — and the champ or points leader likely wins 8–10 games anyway, adding $160–$200 on top.
